Scope: the NSC Garage one-page site (src/App.tsx and its sections). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: drift/competition drivers and owners of sports and imported cars, mostly on phones, deciding whether to trust NSC with their car. Action: start a WhatsApp conversation (form composes the message; floating button; footer number). Proof: the team's own night drift footage, the 350Z #69 livery, Neto as founder, over a decade of results, international titles and the pioneering role in structuring drift in Brazil (confirmed). Constraints: keep the hero video and the 350Z drifting along the S-track in the middle of the page; brand red/black/white; no invented testimonials, title names or statistics; contact data and timeline facts are placeholders.

Memorable moment: the 350Z runs the course down the middle of the page, leaving rubber on the asphalt and lighting each clipping point as it passes; it ends doing donuts in the red footer.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the official run sheet of a drift heat, the paperwork of the sport NSC helped structure in Brazil. It refuses the category default of a black page with neon-red racing caps, icon cards and a centred hero.

OWN-WORLD: Judge's sheet paper (cool light grey-white, never cream), carbon-black ink, brand red as the judge's marker used on the action and the clipping points only, asphalt black for the course, tyre-rubber black for marks. One grotesk (Hanken Grotesk, medium, sentence case, tight tracking) in two sizes. Square corners. Filled blocks, no hairlines outside the form and the table.

STORY: The visitor sees the night run, then the course with NSC's milestones as clipping points, then who runs the shop (Neto, titles, pioneering), what it does (criteria table), and fills the entry form to talk on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Paper ground. Logo top-left, three links and a small solid red "Agendar" button top-right. Centre: a 4:5 window onto the full-bleed night drift video, 36% wide. Left of it, bottom-aligned: h1 "Competir é a obsessão." on two lines at about 64px. Right of it, bottom-aligned: one sentence on what NSC is and the red action. Scrolling opens the window to full screen while the text rises away.

FORM: Drift judging sheet, position 7 of 7 on the ordered list (1 night track, 2 livery, 3 tyre marks and track paint, 4 carbon fibre, 5 setup sheet, 6 event poster and magazine, 7 judging sheet). seed:e1b1cf794a102dde (degraded roll, no challengers; roll service unreachable from this sandbox).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
